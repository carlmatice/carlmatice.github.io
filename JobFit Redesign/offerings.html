What We Offer

Hands-on pathways designed and owned by JobFit.

JobFit coordinates each pathway from learner intake through coaching and placement. Employer partners, including CloudFit Software, support delivery by hosting projects, mentorship, and practical experience.

STEM

Early exploration and career mapping

JobFit partners with local STEM academies, high schools, and Central Virginia Community College to help students explore technology domains and align future coursework with their interests.

Internships

Mentored placements across partner organizations

JobFit delivers hands-on IT internships in partnership with leading employers and organizations, including CloudFit Software. Interns join active teams in software, cybersecurity, IT operations, project management, and UI/UX.

Capstone

Team-based problem solving with real stakeholders

Senior learners collaborate on current technical challenges and produce portfolio-quality solutions while practicing communication, ownership, and delivery discipline.

Internship Ownership

How JobFit internships are structured

Internship operations are managed by JobFit. Host organizations provide work environments and role-specific mentorship, while JobFit provides learner support, quality standards, and pathway continuity.

1. Discovery and readiness screening

Learners are evaluated on commitment, growth mindset, and baseline technical aptitude, then coached into the right pathway.

2. Partner matching and onboarding

JobFit matches learners to host organizations based on goals, project fit, and support capacity.

3. Guided execution and mentorship

Interns receive day-to-day mentoring from host teams, plus structured check-ins and growth plans from JobFit coaches.

4. Reflection, portfolio, and transition

Program completion emphasizes skill articulation, portfolio evidence, interview preparation, and next-step planning.
